# 💊 Hospital Management System (HMS) — Pharmacist Guide & Operations Playbook

## 📋 1. Executive Overview

The Pharmacist role (`PHARMACIST`) manages pharmacy operations, prescription verification, drug dispensing, formulary management, drug-drug interaction safety checks, and controlled substance compliance in the Hospital Management System (HMS).

This guide outlines all pharmacy workflows, inventory integration, controlled substance tracking, endpoint APIs, and pharmacy best practices.

## 💊 3. Core Pharmacy Modules & Workflows

### 3.1 Pending Prescription Queue
* **Queue Ingestion**: Automatically receives digital prescriptions generated by physicians during consultations.
* **Prescription Statuses**:
  * `PENDING`: Awaiting pharmacist verification.
  * `VERIFIED`: Pharmacist approved dosage and safety checks.
  * `DISPENSED`: Medications handed over to patient/nurse with stock deducted.
  * `CANCELLED`: Prescription cancelled or returned for physician clarification.

### 3.2 Clinical Verification & Safety Checks
* **5-Point Prescription Verification**:
  1. Confirm physician signature and active prescribing license.
  2. Verify patient demographics and allergy profile.
  3. Validate drug dosage, administration frequency, and duration.
  4. Perform Automated Drug Interaction Check (e.g., Warfarin + NSAIDs alerts).
  5. Check patient active medication history for therapeutic duplication.

### 3.3 Dispensing & Inventory Deduction
* **Batch & Expiry Allocation**: Select specific inventory batches (FEFO - First Expire, First Out).
* **Automatic Inventory Sync**: Dispensing automatically decrements pharmacy stock levels in the Inventory Module.
* **Label Generation**: Generate prescription bottle labels with patient name, drug dosage, usage instructions, and warning labels (e.g., *"Take with food"*).

### 3.4 Controlled Substance Log & Regulatory Compliance
* **Narcotic Registry**: High-risk drugs (Opioids, Sedatives, Controlled Schedule II-V) require double-pharmacist authorization.
* **Discrepancy Audits**: Real-time logging of controlled substance stock vs. dispensed totals for regulatory audit readiness.

## 🔑 4. Permissions & Role Boundaries

The `PHARMACIST` role is pre-configured with pharmacy operations permissions:

| Permitted Action | Endpoint Scope | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **View Prescriptions** | `GET /api/v1/pharmacy/prescriptions/pending` | Fetch pending prescription queue |
| **Verify Prescription**| `PUT /api/v1/pharmacy/prescriptions/{id}/verify` | Validate prescription safety |
| **Dispense Medication**| `POST /api/v1/pharmacy/dispense` | Dispense & deduct inventory stock |
| **Check Interactions** | `GET /api/v1/pharmacy/drug-interactions` | Query drug-drug interaction matrix |
| **View Drug Catalog** | `GET /api/v1/pharmacy/drugs` | Access formulary drug catalog |
| **Log Narcotic Record**| `POST /api/v1/pharmacy/controlled-substances` | Register controlled substance dispensing |

> ⚠️ **Restricted Actions**: Pharmacists cannot create initial diagnosis notes, alter nursing vital signs, edit billing tariffs, or modify system admin user roles.

## 💡 6. Pharmacy Best Practices

1. **📦 Enforce FEFO**: Always dispense inventory from batches with the earliest expiration date first.
2. **⚠️ Dual Verification for High-Alert Drugs**: Require secondary review for high-risk medications (Insulin, Heparin, Concentrated Electrolytes).
3. **🗣️ Patient Counseling**: Ensure clear oral/written instructions on storage requirements (e.g. refrigeration for Insulin).
